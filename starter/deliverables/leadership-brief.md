# Leadership Brief: Momentum Launch Messaging

To: Brand Manager · From: Marketing Operations · Date: 2026-11-12

## Where we are

We have four ads ready to go. The writing tool drafted 14. A separate checker threw out the ones that broke our rules, and I approved 7 of the rest for a practice test. The four below cover our three strongest messages at the lowest cost per customer.

## What I'd ship

| Ad | How it did in the practice test |
|---|---|
| "See 90 Days of Cash Ahead" (a 90-day forecast built from your real transactions) | Most clicks: about 3.7 of every 100 people who saw it |
| "Locally Owned Since 1999" (nine metro Atlanta locations plus two in Savannah) | About $28 per new customer, among the cheapest |
| "A Named Advisor. No Bots." | Middle of the pack, but our only message about real people. I picked it over a second forecast ad that scored a bit higher but repeated the first. |
| "Cash Flow, Not Guesswork" | Cheapest of everything we tested at $27.34 per new customer. Ships on one condition, below. |

I'd stop the two fee ads, "Starter Is $0 a Month" and "Free ACH and Incoming Wires." They were compliant but came last on every number. The $0 ad cost $63 per new customer, more than double our best.

## Three risks, and what we're doing about each

**The tool overstates where loan decisions happen.** In two separate drafting rounds it wrote that loans are "decided in Atlanta." Our approved claim says decisions are made locally, and we lend in Savannah too. The checker caught it both times, but I don't want to depend on that. The fix is a plain rule with no AI involved: "Atlanta" next to "loan" gets blocked, and only the approved sentence is allowed.

**Some ads are only compliant if the landing page carries the rest.** Five of the first ten drafts need something that won't fit in a 90-character ad: the "Member FDIC" label, the current savings rate and its date, or a note that a feature is on the $49 Pro plan. Nobody checks the landing page today. From now on, each approved ad comes with a list of what the page must show, and it doesn't go live until someone confirms every item.

**"Passed the check" isn't the same as "safe to run."** The checker isn't fully consistent on tone. Run twice on the same drafts, it disagreed on one in ten in the first round. Its character count was also off in two of four runs, because of how it counted a dash. Now the stricter of two runs wins, and a simple script counts length instead of the AI.

## The condition on "Cash Flow, Not Guesswork"

"Not Guesswork" could read as a promise that the forecast is certain, when it's an estimate. Ship it only once the landing page calls it a projection. Good numbers don't settle a compliance question.

## How far to trust the numbers

This was a simulator that rewards an ad's topic, not its wording, and each ad got only 1,300 to 3,700 views. Fee-only messages clearly lose. Local roots and the cash-flow forecast come out about even, so I'd split the budget between them for two weeks before choosing one.

## What I need from you

Approval to run these four ads in a two-week live test with a spending cap, once the landing-page checklist is done. Legal should look at the "Not Guesswork" wording before it goes out.
